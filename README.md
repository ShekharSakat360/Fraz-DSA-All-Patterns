## 🧠 My Problem-Solving Approach

For every problem, I aim to follow a structured approach:

1. **Understand the Problem:** Identify inputs, outputs, constraints, and edge cases.
2. **Think Independently:** Try to develop the solution without immediately looking at hints.
3. **Brute Force:** Understand the straightforward approach first.
4. **Optimize:** Explore better approaches using suitable DSA patterns.
5. **Pattern Recognition:** Identify the underlying concept and why it works.
6. **Implementation:** Write a clean and readable C++ solution.
7. **Complexity Analysis:** Calculate time and space complexity.
8. **Revision:** Revisit difficult problems and attempt them again independently.
